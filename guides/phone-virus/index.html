Nuke (almost) any virus off your phone.

Every step and every tool from the video, in order. Start at level one and stop as soon as the problem is gone. Do this on your own phone only.

Stop first if you think someone you know put stalkerware on your phone (a partner or an ex). Removing it can alert them and delete evidence. Contact a support service and make a safety plan first, using a different device: Coalition Against Stalkerware.

LEVEL 1Find and remove the bad app

Most infections are an app you installed yourself. Five minutes.

  • ANDROIDBoot into Safe Mode. If the pop-ups stop, uninstall your newest apps until they're gone.
  • ANDROIDApp won't uninstall? Settings → Security → Device admin apps: remove its permission, then delete it.
  • ANDROIDSettings → Accessibility: turn off anything you don't recognize (it can read your screen and tap for you).
  • BOTHBlock website notifications you don't know: Chrome site notifications.
  • IPHONEDelete spam calendar subscriptions (the fake "virus alert" events).
  • IPHONESettings → General → VPN & Device Management: remove any profile you didn't install.

LEVEL 2Second opinion and your accounts

No single scanner catches everything, and sometimes the phone is fine but the account isn't.

  • ANDROIDRun a Play Protect scan, then a free scan with Malwarebytes.
  • IPHONEDon't pay for "antivirus" apps: iOS doesn't let apps scan other apps.
  • BOTHWorried about real spyware: iVerify Basic, or Amnesty's free Mobile Verification Toolkit (GitHub).
  • BOTHSign out devices you don't recognize: Google devices · Google Security Checkup · Apple Account devices.
  • BOTHWhatsApp → Settings → Linked devices: log out anything that isn't yours.
  • BOTHChange your passwords from a computer you trust.

LEVEL 3Factory reset, the right way

  • Back up photos, contacts and files, not apps.
  • Reset the phone, set it up as new.
  • Install updates first, then fresh apps from the official store.

A reset doesn't touch the system partition. If it's back, go to level 4.

LEVEL 4Reflash the firmware

  • PIXELAndroid Flash Tool (wipes everything; relock the bootloader after).
  • SAMSUNGSmart Switch recovery, or a service center.
  • IPHONERecovery mode → Restore.
  • No official firmware for your cheap no-name phone? Stop using it.

LEVEL 5If you have real enemies

  • BOTHRestart your phone every week (the NSA's mobile best practices).
  • IPHONETurn on Lockdown Mode. Know what a real Apple threat notification looks like (it never asks you to click or install anything).
  • ANDROID 16Turn on Advanced Protection.
  • BOTHJournalists and activists: Amnesty Security Lab · Access Now Digital Security Helpline (free).

ALWAYSWhat actually protects you

  • Keep your phone updated. That alone stopped the Play Store attack from the video. No updates anymore? A supported phone fixes more than any app.
  • Buying Android? Check it's Play Protect certified. Avoid weirdly cheap no-name boxes (FBI warning on BadBox 2.0).
  • "Your phone has 13 viruses" pop-ups are ads, and the "cleaner" they push is the risk.
